# Artist Reference

## Neon Echo
A versatile act straddling pop and synthwave. Their pop output ("Sunrise City") is bright, uplifting, and radio-ready, while their synthwave side ("Night Drive Loop") leans moody and nocturnal. Strong 2020s production sensibility.

## LoRoom
A lofi hip-hop producer focused on study and focus beats. High instrumentalness, mellow keys, tempos in the 75-85 BPM range. Fans of Nujabes or Jinsang will feel at home. Catalog tracks: "Midnight Coding," "Focus Flow."

## Voltline
A 2010s/2020s hard-rock act with high-energy, guitar-forward production. Tempos north of 150 BPM, aggressive mood tags, minimal acousticness. Two catalog tracks: "Storm Runner" and "Digital Fury."

## Paper Lanterns
An acoustic-leaning act that spans lofi and folk. Their lofi cut ("Library Rain") is peaceful and dreamy with very high acousticness; their folk cut ("Autumn Letters") is bittersweet and nostalgic. Great for study and quiet evenings.

## Max Pulse
An energetic act with a foot in both pop and edm. "Gym Hero" is euphoric and aggressive workout pop; "Neon Bounce" is pure edm for parties. Consistent high danceability and high popularity.

## Orbit Bloom
An ambient artist making slow, textural music. Very low energy, very high acousticness, very high instrumentalness. Good for sleep, meditation, or deep focus. Two modes in the catalog: peaceful ("Spacewalk Thoughts") and brooding ("Ghost Frequencies").

## Slow Stereo
A smooth jazz act evoking late nights and coffee shops. Warm chord voicings, brushed drums, moderate tempo, very high acousticness. "Coffee Shop Stories" and "Blue Note Cafe" cover the 2000s and 1990s ends respectively.

## Indigo Parade
An indie pop band with warm, uplifting production. Energy around 0.75, moderate acousticness, happy moods. "Rooftop Lights" is the signature track — 2020s indie pop at its most sun-drenched.

## DJ Vertigo
A festival-oriented edm producer. Very high energy (0.95+), very high danceability, euphoric and aggressive mood tags, elevated liveness for that festival-floor feel. "Bass Cathedral" is the catalog entry.

## Dusty Strings
A country act with a melancholy, storytelling bent. Acoustic guitar, lap steel, moderate tempo, bittersweet mood tags. "Lonely Highway" is an emotional highway drive track.

## Iron Veil
A metal band with fast tempos, aggressive mood tags, and elevated liveness. "Rage Circuit" pushes past 170 BPM and is the catalog's heaviest track.

## Luna Haze
An R&B artist with smooth vocal textures, groove-forward production, and romantic or sensual mood tags. "Velvet Daydream" is the warm, romantic signature.

## Ritmo Vivo
A latin act with very high danceability, high valence, warm and euphoric mood tags, and elevated liveness. "Salsa del Sol" is a sunlit dance floor track.
