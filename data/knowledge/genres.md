# Genre Reference

## pop
Mainstream pop is built on catchy hooks, vocal-forward production, and tempos in the 100-130 BPM range. It tends to score high on valence and danceability, low on acousticness, and skews toward the 2010s-2020s. Pop listeners usually want polished, familiar-sounding tracks with clear emotional direction. In this catalog Neon Echo and Max Pulse are the main pop/adjacent acts.

## rock
Rock ranges from mid-energy indie to high-energy hard rock. Driven by electric guitars, live-sounding drums, and tempos from 110 BPM up past 160 BPM for heavier subgenres. Rock tracks typically have low acousticness, elevated liveness, and intense or aggressive mood tags. Voltline and Iron Veil represent the heavier end of rock in this catalog.

## lofi
Lofi hip hop prioritizes warmth and imperfection: soft drums, dusty samples, mellow keys, and tempos between 70-90 BPM. High instrumentalness, medium-to-high acousticness, and low liveness are hallmarks. Listeners reach for lofi when they want to focus, relax, or study without vocals demanding attention. LoRoom and Paper Lanterns make lofi in this catalog.

## jazz
Jazz in this catalog is smooth and mellow rather than bebop. Think warm chord voicings, brushed drums, acoustic bass, and moderate tempos. High acousticness, low energy, and warm/nostalgic mood tags are typical. Good for dinner, coffee shops, and reflective moods. Slow Stereo is the main jazz act here.

## edm
Electronic dance music is built for movement: four-on-the-floor kicks, heavy synthesis, tempos from 120-140 BPM, and very high danceability and energy. Low acousticness, often high liveness (festival feel), and euphoric/aggressive mood tags. DJ Vertigo and Max Pulse represent edm in the catalog.

## ambient
Ambient music is texture over rhythm: slow-moving pads, field recordings, minimal drums. Very low energy, very high acousticness, very high instrumentalness. Used for meditation, sleep, or as background for deep work. Orbit Bloom's tracks in this catalog are prime examples.

## synthwave
Synthwave revives 1980s synth textures: gated drums, arpeggiated basslines, ethereal pads. Mid-energy, moody, nostalgic. Sits between pop and electronic. Neon Echo's "Night Drive Loop" is the example in this catalog.

## country
Country in this catalog leans melancholic and acoustic — think lap steel, acoustic guitar, vocal storytelling at moderate tempos. High acousticness, lower valence, bittersweet and nostalgic mood tags. Dusty Strings is the representative act.

## folk
Folk is acoustic and intimate: fingerpicked guitar, soft vocals, sparse arrangement. Very high acousticness, low energy, nostalgic and peaceful mood tags. Paper Lanterns crosses over from lofi into folk territory.

## metal
Metal pushes rock further: distorted guitars, double-kick drums, tempos often above 160 BPM, very high energy, aggressive mood tags, elevated liveness for that live-show intensity. Iron Veil is the metal act in this catalog.

## r&b
R&B is groove-forward: syncopated drums, smooth vocals, warm basslines, tempos 80-100 BPM. Moderate energy, high danceability, romantic or sensual moods. Luna Haze's "Velvet Daydream" is the example here.

## latin
Latin music in this catalog is high-energy and danceable — salsa, latin pop, reggaeton-adjacent. Very high danceability, high valence, warm and euphoric mood tags, elevated liveness. Ritmo Vivo is the latin act.
