# Mood Tag Reference

Mood tags are fine-grained descriptors that sit underneath the coarse mood labels (happy, chill, intense, etc.). A song can have multiple mood tags — "Sunrise City" is both "uplifting" and "euphoric."

## uplifting
Bright, major-key, forward-moving. Evokes progress, hope, or triumph. Common in pop, indie pop, and some edm. Pairs naturally with high valence and moderate-to-high energy.

## euphoric
A step above uplifting — peaks of joy, release, ecstasy. Think festival drops, anthemic pop choruses, or gospel-infused R&B. High valence, high energy, often high liveness.

## mellow
Soft, relaxed, unhurried. Low energy, medium valence, often high acousticness. Good for winding down, reading, or light background.

## dreamy
Ethereal, washed-out, contemplative. Reverb-heavy, slow-moving harmonies. Found in lofi, ambient, and some indie pop. Pairs with high instrumentalness and moderate acousticness.

## aggressive
Forceful, confrontational, energy-forward. Distorted guitars or hard-hitting drums. Common in metal, hard rock, edm drops, and intense pop. Pairs with high energy and low valence (or defiant valence).

## powerful
Strong and commanding without necessarily being angry. Epic, cinematic, driving. Rock, metal, and some orchestral electronic. Overlaps with aggressive but less hostile.

## peaceful
Calm, serene, meditative. Very low energy, medium-to-high valence, very high acousticness. Common in ambient, folk, and some lofi.

## ethereal
Otherworldly, spacious, weightless. Common in ambient and synthwave. Very high instrumentalness and acousticness, low liveness.

## warm
Inviting, intimate, human. Acoustic textures, analog synths, close-mic vocals. Spans jazz, indie pop, r&b, and folk. Pairs with moderate acousticness and high valence.

## nostalgic
Evokes memory, longing, the past. Often bittersweet — happy chord progressions with a tinge of sadness. Country, folk, jazz, and some indie pop lean nostalgic.

## bittersweet
Mixed emotions — sadness and warmth together. Medium-low valence with major-key sensibilities. Country, folk, and some pop ballads.

## brooding
Dark, introspective, heavy-hearted. Low valence, minor keys, slow-to-medium tempos. Ambient, synthwave, and some jazz can brood.

## focused
Minimal distraction, steady rhythm, low emotional peaks. Lofi's signature mode. Low-to-medium energy, instrumental, consistent tempo. Good for coding, studying, reading.

## sensual
Slow, groovy, intimate. R&B's signature mode. Moderate energy, syncopated rhythms, smooth vocals, warm production.
